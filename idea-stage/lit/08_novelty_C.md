## A. Lineage-transferable monitors (ideas 1 and 4 merged)

**Proposed method.** Fit hallucination/correctness probes and refusal/steering directions on a parent (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct or the Qwen2.5-7B base). Reuse them on descendants (R1-Distill-Qwen-7B, L2S-merged models, AWQ/INT4 versions, Dream-7B) after only unlabeled mean/covariance re-centering.

**Core claims and closest work**
1. *Probes and directions survive post-training within a lineage.* Closest: **2504.02904** (How Post-Training Reshapes LLMs). The truthfulness direction is nearly identical between base and post-trained models. Base→instruct probe transfer shows ~0 Δ accuracy on Llama-3.1-8B, Mistral-7B and Llama-2-13B, and transferred interventions work. **The refusal direction differs between base and post-trained models: forward (base→instruct) transfer is limited; only backward transfer works.** **2606.15821** (The Truth Stays in the Family, ICML 2026): head-level context-truthfulness scores are strongly preserved from Qwen2.5-7B to Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-VL and Qwen2.5-Omni, and from Vicuna to LLaVA. Base-LLM scores used as a soft gate (TruthProbe) improve HaluEval, POPE and CHAIR on descendants. The paper attributes this to small attention-weight drift within a family. Earlier and well known: Kissane et al. 2024 (SAEs transfer between base and chat; Alignment Forum, not on arXiv) and 2410.12877 (instruct→base steering). → **Claim 1 is no longer novel for base→instruct/MLLM.**
2. *Transfer extends to heavy-post-training descendants: reasoning distillation (≈800k SFT traces), L2S merges, AR→diffusion conversion.* Nothing found that contains this. 2608.07430 shows safety *neurons* are inherited by Dream/LLaDA and uses that for attacks. 2603.07475 is similarity analysis only. 2602.09434 uses refusal vectors as fingerprints across 76 offspring models, including merges. That shows directional persistence but not monitor performance. → **open.**
3. *Unlabeled re-centering (translation and scale) recovers same-model AUROC.* No paper does label-free re-centering for cross-checkpoint monitors. **2609.30275** (Cosine Similarity Is Not Evidence, Aug 2026) is directly relevant to the method. INT4 quantization measurably *rotates* the refusal direction beyond the estimator's noise floor. Scale-invariant statistics (cosine, AUROC) cannot tell translation from attenuation, so evaluation must report the split-half noise floor. 2609.06473 covers steering × quantization. → the method is novel, but the **quantization arm is already partly covered** and must use 2609.30275's null.
4. *Cross-model transfer needs learned maps.* 2503.04429, 2506.19140 (Command-V), 2610.09058 (MATCHA), 2605.09875 (anchor projection) and 2608.05164 (cross-architecture SAE steering) all *learn* a mapping between different models. Our setting is zero-map, same-lineage, with no labels. That is a different regime, but reviewers will ask for a Command-V or affine-map baseline.

**Facts that change the design**
- **DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B is fine-tuned from Qwen2.5-Math-7B, not from Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct.** Instruct→R1-distill is therefore a *cousin* transfer (Qwen2.5-7B → {Instruct, Math→R1-distill}). The 2503.20641 L2S merge combines Qwen2.5-Math-7B with R1-Distill-Qwen-7B. Dream-7B is initialised from the Qwen2.5-7B base. The parent is best taken to be the Qwen2.5-7B base or Qwen2.5-Math-7B, with Instruct as a sibling.
- 2504.02904 found refusal forward transfer limited (base→instruct). That predicts refusal-direction transfer will be the weak arm and truthfulness/correctness the strong arm. This is a falsifiable prediction to test.

**Verdict: PROCEED WITH CAUTION.** The delta is thin because 2606.15821 and 2504.02904 already establish within-lineage inheritance of truth signals and transferable probes. **What would make it carry:** (i) results on the descendants those papers do not cover: long-CoT distillation, L2S merges and AR→dLLM, with the answer position aligned after `</think>` or at the masked answer position for Dream. (ii) A mechanism with a predictive rule. Decompose the drift into translation, scale and rotation (per 2609.30275), and show that re-centering fixes translation while rotation, predicted by weight-delta norm or CKA, marks where transfer fails. (iii) Baselines: same-model probe (upper bound), raw transfer, Command-V/affine map with 200 unlabeled prompts, and first-token entropy (lower bound).
**Delta (one sentence):** within-lineage probe and direction transfer is known for base→instruct/MLLM (2504.02904, 2606.15821); we test reasoning-distilled, merged and diffusion-converted descendants, and show that label-free re-centering recovers ≥90% of same-model AUROC unless the descendant's monitor direction rotates (measured against a split-half null).
**Cheapest pilot (≈0.5 GPU-day, one A100 or 4090):** Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct → {Qwen2.5-Math-7B, R1-Distill-Qwen-7B, Dream-v0-Instruct-7B}. Use TriviaQA plus SciQ correctness labels (2k questions, greedy). Fit a per-layer L2-regularized logistic-regression probe on the parent at the matched answer position. Report raw / re-centered / native AUROC and the cosine between parent and native directions against a split-half null. **Kill:** if re-centered AUROC is <0.75 of native AUROC on R1-distill *and* Dream, or if raw transfer already reaches ≥95%, so re-centering adds nothing and 2606.15821 already covers the story.

## B. Safety cost of long-to-short merging + refusal-direction-preserving merge (idea 2)

**Full-text checks.** **2503.20641**: no safety, harm, jailbreak or refusal evaluation anywhere. Its models are Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B/7B/14B/32B (a *base* math model with no safety tuning) merged with R1-distill, evaluated on GSM8K, MATH500, AIME24 and so on. **2510.10977**: no safety evaluation. It uses Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 and Thinking-2507 on AIME'25, IFEval and GPQA, and describes three-stage interpolation dynamics.

**Core claims and closest work**
1. *L2S merging changes safety.* **2604.04120** (Shorter, but Still Trustworthy?, COLM 2026) evaluates CoT-compression methods for safety (HarmBench, AgentHarm), hallucination and multilingual ability. As supplementary comparisons it includes 2503.20641-style merged checkpoints on a DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B base. HarmBench is 71.5 at baseline; average and TA merges reach 75.2, while TIES, DARE-TIES and TIES-origin merges reach 62.0–64.5. The paper concludes that "merging-based variants exhibit substantial variation in safety outcomes across merging strategies". → **The headline "L2S merging silently changes safety" is partly taken**, though only at one coefficient per method, with static prompts and no mechanism.
2. *Safety vs. α is non-monotonic, worst at mid α.* Not found anywhere. 2608.08542 sweeps λ ∈ {0.2..1.0}, but for skill merges (math, code, uncensored) into Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Llama-3.1 and Mistral. Benign math merges made models *safer* under static tests yet much more fragile under adaptive attacks (GCG, PAIR, template). There is no thinking↔instruct interpolation. → **open.**
3. *Mechanism: the shortened CoT plus a refusal direction misaligned with both endpoints.* Related findings: 2605.26772 (CoT reconstructs refusal in R1-Distill-Llama-8B) and 2606.25013 (refusal/compliance is decodable from the first token at 0.84–0.95 AUROC, and thinking behaves like prefix completion). The latter *weakens* the "shortened CoT removes deliberation" story. → open, but the hypothesis should shift to "refusal readout at the first token versus α".
4. *Training-free repair.* **2608.08542 SubSafe-Merge** projects the task vector off a weight-space safety subspace estimated from an aligned/abliterated pair. AlignMerge 2512.16245 adds a Fisher plus alignment-subspace constraint. 2608.26506 shows suffixes that transfer across merge coefficients within a family. → our repair (activation-space refusal direction, layer-wise fallback by refusal-direction cosine) must beat SubSafe-Merge head-to-head.
5. *Caveat from 2609.23587* (Efficiency–Safety Dilemma in LRMs: quantization, pruning, KV compression, but not merging). An apparent drop in ASR can be "attempted but failed" harmful output caused by capability loss. Merged models need a capability-adjusted harm metric.

**Verdict: PROCEED WITH CAUTION.** The delta is thin because 2604.04120 already reports that L2S merges move HarmBench by −9.5 to +3.7 pts, and 2608.08542 already covers λ-sweeps, adaptive attacks and a projection repair for skill merges. **What would make it carry:** the full α-curve (11 points) for thinking↔instruct interpolation under *adaptive* attacks (at least PAIR plus one template attack), and over-refusal (XSTest). Add a mechanistic readout (first-token refusal probe and refusal-direction projection versus α). Finally, a repair that beats SubSafe-Merge on the safety–capability Pareto front, scored with an "attempted but failed" correction.
**Delta:** first α-resolved, adaptive-attack safety curve for instruct↔thinking interpolation, explained by a refusal readout and repaired training-free better than SubSafe-Merge.
**Testbed fix:** use **Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 × Qwen3-4B-Thinking-2507**, which share a base and are the 2510.10977 setting. Do not use Qwen2.5-Math-7B × R1-distill, because the Math "short" end has no safety tuning and the merge-to-safety claim would be confounded.
**Cheapest pilot (≈0.5 GPU-day, vLLM on one 80GB card):** linear interpolation at α ∈ {0, 0.2, …, 1}. Evaluate HarmBench-200 plus StrongREJECT-100 under static prompts and PAIR (20 iterations on 50 behaviors), XSTest, and MATH500-200 accuracy and length, with Llama-Guard-3-8B and HarmBench-cls under an AND judging rule. **Kill:** if adaptive ASR is monotone in α and lies within ±5 pts of linear interpolation between the endpoints, there is no hidden cost. Write it up as a negative note, or fold it into A as a monitor-robustness section.

## C. Audit of 2024-era training-free MLLM hallucination methods on 2026 MLLMs (idea 3)

**Core claims and closest work**
1. *CD methods' POPE/CHAIR gains are spurious.* **2504.10020** (The Mirage of Performance Gains, NeurIPS 2025): VCD, ICD and SID gains on POPE come from a one-directional output shift and from the adaptive plausibility constraint turning sampling into greedy decoding. Spurious "always lean Yes" baselines match them. That work is mostly on LLaVA-1.5.
2. *…and this still holds on 2026 MLLMs.* **2607.25196** (Rethinking the Effectiveness of CD, Jul 2026) runs VCD, ICD and SID on **LLaVA-1.5-7B, Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct and InternVL3-8B**. Logit-lens analysis shows the contrastive shift is unrelated to hallucination status (VCD AUC 0.523). A constant shift beats all three on POPE, and greedy decoding beats every CD method on CHAIR and LLaVA-Bench for all three models. → **The CD part of idea 3 is contained in 2607.25196.** Do not re-run it as a contribution; cite it and use its controls.
3. *CD discards valid abstentions.* 2610.16722 (Lost in Contrast; 9 models, decision utility). → taken.
4. *Non-CD families (PAI-style attention amplification, OPERA beam penalty, DoLa-style layer contrast, head-level and representation steering such as VTI, ICT and AOD 2605.25377) give little or nothing on Qwen2.5/3-VL and InternVL3 once matched-compute greedy, prompt and resolution controls are applied, and the remaining gain shrinks with base-model strength.* Not found. New methods (2606.20419, 2608.28058, 2608.09344) do report on Qwen2.5-VL, but none audits the older methods under 2607.25196-style controls. → **open, but narrower.**

**Verdict: PROCEED WITH CAUTION (narrowed).** The delta is thin because the most-cited half (CD) is already done on exactly the target models. **What would make it carry:** limit the audit to non-CD interventions; adopt 2607.25196's controls (greedy, constant-shift, magnitude-matched random perturbation) plus 2610.16722's decision utility; add Qwen3-VL-8B and one reasoning MLLM. Above all, report a *predictive* finding, such as gain versus a measured language-prior strength across 4–6 models, rather than a leaderboard. This is a TMLR or benchmarks-track paper, not a methods paper.
**Delta:** first controlled audit of attention, beam and steering hallucination interventions on 2026 MLLMs, extending 2607.25196's CD-only finding to the non-CD half of the field.
**Cheapest pilot (≈0.5 GPU-day):** PAI and OPERA (official code) on Qwen2.5-VL-7B and InternVL3-8B. Use CHAIR-500 and POPE-adversarial, comparing greedy, sampling, greedy + method and greedy + magnitude-matched random attention scaling. **Kill:** if PAI or OPERA keeps ≥2 CHAIR-i points over *greedy* and beats random scaling on both models, the "they stop working" thesis fails. It then becomes a positive replication, with less interest.

## D. Early-window-only visual contrastive guidance for diffusion VLMs (idea 5)

**Full text of DynaHall (2609.34772).** Across 5 dVLMs, an unsupported answer is already preferred while the answer position is still masked, and later steps rarely reverse it. **The paper already proposes PGS**, a pre-commitment gradient steering method. PGS edits head activations at the masked answer position only while it is masked, gated by the image. On MMaDA-8B-Base it cuts FP from 42.7% to 28.5% and gains 3.0 accuracy points, with transfer to Dimple-7B and capability checks on POPE, MME and CHAIR. **Baselines VCD and DoLa *increase* false positives on dVLMs**, because amplifying the image-conditional distribution worsens over-affirmation. The paper cites concurrent VISAGE (2603.25711), which re-ranks token commitments using cross-attention spatial entropy.

**Core claims and closest work**
1. *Image-conditional CFG in dMLLMs.* **2604.05497** (Thinking Diffusion: PSP + VRG). VRG is CFG between the logits with the image and without it, applied at every step, on LaViDa, MMaDA and others. It is evaluated on M3CoT, ScienceQA, MMBench and V\*, not on hallucination benchmarks. Its finding is that dMLLMs show *low* visual dependence in early steps and commit answers early. → taken as a method; the early-window restriction is not.
2. *Guidance value is front-loaded; freezing CFG early is noninferior.* **2608.08082** (Commitment Before Realization; LLaDA-Instruct, 13 constrained-generation subtasks, text-only). → the text analogue of our hypothesis is shown.
3. *Pre-commitment-window intervention against dVLM hallucination.* DynaHall's PGS. → taken for activation steering; not for guidance.
4. *Early-window visual guidance keeps ≥90% of full-step guidance's hallucination gain at about k% of its extra cost.* Not found. But DynaHall's result that VCD hurts suggests full-step guidance may have **no** gain to preserve on over-affirming dVLMs. 2609.01318 meanwhile reports that dLVLMs *reverse* the AR yes-bias on standard binary queries. The sign of the effect depends on the model and benchmark, and this is unresolved.

**Verdict: PROCEED WITH CAUTION.** The delta is thin: it is the visual and hallucination analogue of 2608.08082, it restricts 2604.05497's VRG to a time window, and it competes with PGS in the same window. **What would make it carry:** (i) show that full-step VRG or VCD *does* reduce hallucination on at least one dVLM and benchmark (LLaDA-V on POPE or AMBER), then show the early window keeps that gain at ≤25% of the extra forward passes. (ii) Explain DynaHall's finding that VCD hurts, for example as a sign/prior issue fixed by a blank-image versus perturbed-image reference or by abstention-preserving guidance (2610.16722). (iii) Compare head-to-head with PGS at matched compute.
**Delta:** first step-windowed visual guidance for dVLM hallucination, matching full-step guidance at a fraction of the cost, and reconciling DynaHall's result that VCD hurts.
**Cheapest pilot (≈0.3 GPU-day):** LLaDA-V-8B; POPE-adversarial (3k) and AMBER-disc; 32 steps; VRG/VCD with k ∈ {0, 10, 25, 50, 100}% of steps; track the affirmation rate. **Kill:** if full-step guidance (k=100) does not improve POPE-adversarial F1 by ≥1 pt over vanilla, there is nothing to window. Pivot to "why guidance hurts in dVLMs", or drop the idea.
