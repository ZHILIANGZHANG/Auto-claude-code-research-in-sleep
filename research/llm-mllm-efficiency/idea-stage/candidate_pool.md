## C1. Draftability tax: length-compressed CoT is harder to speculatively decode
*Proposed independently by S1, S2, S3 (3/3 shards).*
- **Method**: Take public verbose/compressed pairs on the same base (R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B/7B vs L1, ThinkPrune, AdaptThink, LC-R1). Generate traces on MATH-500/AIME; measure speculative acceptance length τ with (i) model-free n-gram / suffix-lookup drafting (drafter-free, exact under greedy), (ii) a fixed small same-family drafter, (iii) EAGLE-3 where a head exists. Separate drafter-target distribution shift (fixable by re-distilling the drafter on compressed traces) from intrinsic loss of predictable tokens. Report vLLM wall-clock per solved problem and "effective compression" = wall-clock ratio / token ratio.
- **Hypothesis**: Length-penalty RL mostly deletes low-entropy filler ("wait, let me double-check", restated equations) that SD accepts almost for free, so τ drops (e.g. 2.5 → 1.8) and the latency gain is clearly below the token gain. Null (τ unchanged or up) → compression and SD are multiplicative; equally publishable.
- **MVE**: R1-Distill-1.5B vs ThinkPrune-1.5B / L1-1.5B, 200 MATH-500, offline teacher-forced acceptance + vLLM n-gram SD. ~1 GPU-h.
- **Type / risk / effort**: empirical + diagnostic; MEDIUM (drafter shift may explain everything → "just retrain the drafter"); days–3 weeks.
- **prior_work**: none found directly. Nearest: per-domain acceptance 2604.14682; DREAM-R 2605.28678 (draft-alignment reward in RL); SD Performance-or-Illusion 2601.11580.
- **so_what**: every efficient-reasoning paper reports tokens; if tokens ≠ time under SD, the field's headline metric is miscalibrated. Either answer changes how CoT compression is reported.
- **effort_note**: mostly inference; checkpoints public.

## C2. Thinking MLLMs pay a larger visual-token-pruning penalty (late visual evidence)
*S1 + S2.*
- **Method**: Matched Qwen3-VL-{2,4,8}B Instruct vs Thinking. Prune once at prefill (random, FastV-style, DART-style, downsampling) at 50/25/11%. Evaluate MathVista, MathVerse-vision-only, ChartQA, MMMU + simple VQA control. Log per-decode-step visual attention; compute "late-evidence rate" (attention mass / causal effect on tokens a prefill selector dropped) vs CoT position; sweep thinking budget 1K/4K/16K.
- **Hypothesis**: Thinking variants lose 2–3x more relative accuracy at equal keep rate; loss concentrated in long traces whose late look-backs hit dropped tokens; prunability falls as thinking budget grows. Alternative (thinking compensates for missing tokens) also publishable.
- **MVE**: Qwen3-VL-4B Instruct vs Thinking, random + DART at 25%, 150–300 MathVista items, 4K cap. ~2 GPU-h.
- **Type / risk / effort**: empirical + diagnostic; MEDIUM (Instruct vs Thinking differ beyond reasoning length — mitigate within-Thinking budget sweep and both-correct subset); weeks.
- **prior_work**: no Instruct-vs-Thinking pruning comparison found. Nearest: DSTP 2604.12358 (decode-stage shift), V-Skip 2601.13879, VIG 2608.21883.
- **so_what**: thinking MLLMs are where deployment is heading; the 20+ 2026 pruning papers were validated on short-answer models.

## C3. MoE expert prune vs merge is a pass@1 vs pass@k question
*S1 + S2.*
- **Method**: One MoE (OLMoE-1B-7B / DeepSeek-V2-Lite pilot; Qwen3-30B-A3B main), one calibration set, one expert clustering. "Prune" keeps most salient expert per cluster (REAP); "merge" = frequency-weighted average (REAM-style). 25%/50% reduction. Evaluate pass@1…pass@64 on AIME/MATH/LiveCodeBench, MC accuracy, entropy, distinct solutions; in- vs off-domain calibration; attribute pass@k loss to problems whose correct samples used low-frequency experts.
- **Hypothesis**: Tie at pass@1; curves diverge with k (direction to be determined — pruning deletes tail experts carrying rare solutions, or merging collapses routes into mode-seeking averages). Explains REAP vs REAM disagreement as evaluating different points of the curve.
- **MVE**: OLMoE 50%, 150 MATH-500 × 16–32 samples + 200 MMLU. ~2 GPU-h.
- **Type / risk / effort**: empirical (resolves contradiction); MEDIUM (small MoEs may lack a meaningful tail); weeks.
- **prior_work**: REAP 2510.13999, REAM 2604.04356 evaluate greedy; Expert-Sample 2602.02443 warns pass@n only for dynamic top-k.
- **so_what**: settles a live dispute with a deployment rule (prune for greedy serving, merge for TTS or vice versa).

## C4. KV eviction on the few softmax layers of hybrid (linear+softmax) models
*S2 + S3.*
- **Method**: Size-matched hybrid vs dense (Nemotron-H-8B vs Llama-3.1-8B/Qwen3-8B; Falcon-H1-7B; Mamba-in-the-Llama distills vs their parents). Apply identical eviction (sink+window, SnapKV, random, Ada-KV/PyramidKV budgets) only to softmax-layer KV at 5–50%. RULER/NIAH 8K–128K + LongBench; profile per-layer recency mass and attention entropy.
- **Hypothesis**: Hybrid softmax layers have low recency mass / sharp retrieval attention → window eviction collapses, scoring eviction keeps more per byte than on dense, dense budget schedules are wrong-shaped; failures cliff-like (super-additive). Alternative: SSM offloads local context so eviction is ~free.
- **MVE**: Nemotron-H-8B vs Llama-3.1-8B, profiling + SnapKV/window/random at 10% on RULER-32K NIAH. ~2 GPU-h.
- **Type / risk / effort**: empirical + diagnostic; MEDIUM (few softmax layers → modest memory savings; custom cache hooks; distilled hybrids already weak at recall); weeks.
- **prior_work**: only DeltaS 2609.27470 (hybrid *video* eviction via linear state). None for text hybrids.
- **so_what**: production long-context models (Qwen3-Next, Kimi Linear, MiniMax) are hybrids; nobody knows whether to run eviction on them.

## C5. CoT compression kills the random-eviction free lunch
*S1.*
- **Method**: Verbose vs length-compressed pairs (as C1). Random decode-KV eviction (prompt protected) vs R-KV vs SnapKV-style at budgets defined relative to each model's trace length and as absolute counts. Measure trace redundancy (n-gram restatement rate, repeated intermediate results). Regress random-vs-scorer gap on redundancy.
- **Hypothesis**: Random ≈ scorer on verbose models (as Random Attention reports) but random drops sharply on compressed models; compression + eviction savings are sub-additive.
- **MVE**: R1-Distill-1.5B vs L1-1.5B-Max, 100 MATH-500, random vs R-KV at 10%/25%. ~2 GPU-h.
- **Type / risk / effort**: empirical + diagnostic; MEDIUM (relative-budget fairness); weeks.
- **prior_work**: Random Attention 2609.03430, R-KV 2505.24133, DMS 2506.05345; no compressed-model test found.
- **so_what**: explains *why* random works in decode KV and whether the two most popular reasoning-efficiency techniques compete for the same redundancy.

## C6. Fork-token KL predicts test-time-scaling damage across compression families
*S1.*
- **Method**: ~10 compressed variants of one reasoner (layer drop 1/2/4, SparseGPT 50% and 2:4, AWQ/GPTQ 4/3-bit, TEAL 40%). Teacher-force dense model's traces; compute PPL, mean KL, and fork KL (KL at top-10% dense-entropy positions). Ground truth: pass@1, pass@16, output length. Rank-correlate.
- **Hypothesis**: Fork KL predicts pass@k loss and length growth across families (ρ > 0.8) while PPL and mean KL do not; layer drop has outsized fork KL at equal PPL, resolving 2510.22228 vs 2604.25098.
- **MVE**: R1-Distill-1.5B, 5 variants, 200 traces KL + 100 MATH × 8 samples. ~2 GPU-h.
- **Type / risk / effort**: diagnostic; MEDIUM (2606.00206 already shows quantization KL concentrates at high-entropy tokens — novelty rests on cross-family prediction); weeks.
- **so_what**: a cheap, label-free compression-acceptance metric for reasoning models.

## C7. Random-draw headroom: how much can any token selector gain?
*S1.*
- **Method**: For each item and budget, evaluate R=16 random keep-sets (visual tokens in MLLMs; prompt KV in LLMs). Headroom H(b) = acc(best-of-R random) − acc(mean random); items with 0<p_i<1 are "selection-sensitive". Report fraction of headroom each published selector captures; release selection-sensitive items as a discriminative hard subset.
- **Hypothesis**: H(b) ≈ 0 on standard benchmarks at moderate budgets (explains random-is-strong without method stories); headroom concentrated in OCR / grounding / needle items and grows below a budget threshold; current scorers capture < half at low budgets.
- **MVE**: Qwen2.5-VL-3B, TextVQA/GQA/RefCOCO 300 each × 16 draws × 2 budgets + DART/VisionZip. ~1.5 GPU-h.
- **Type / risk / effort**: diagnostic + benchmark; LOW–MEDIUM (best-of-R is only a lower bound on oracle); days–weeks.
- **prior_work**: VTC-Bench 2510.07143, UniPruneBench 2511.02650 (hard subsets by downsampling, not by selection sensitivity); 2609.13012 compares visual KV proxies vs random.
- **so_what**: gives the crowded pruning subfield a precise target and a way to show a gain is real.

## C8. Importance stationarity predicts when scoring beats random (KV + visual tokens)
*S2.*
- **Method**: Define stationarity S = rank correlation / top-B overlap between selection-time importance and oracle use-time importance (attention mass, value-norm-weighted) from all later queries. Compute S for visual tokens (Qwen2.5-VL / LLaVA-OV on VQA/OCR/reasoning) and prompt/decode KV (R1-Distill-7B, Qwen3-8B on LongBench/AIME). Regress Δ(score − random) on S and budget/support size; test whether S explains Random-Attention regime, information horizon, query-visibility inflation.
- **Hypothesis**: Δ ≈ linear in S; random parity exactly where S is low (deep layers, decode KV, multi-turn reuse). If S fails, redundancy (not stationarity) is the cause — also useful.
- **MVE**: Qwen2.5-VL-3B, 500 mixed items, S for FastV/DART at layers 2/8/16/24, 25% vs random. < 2 GPU-h.
- **Type / risk / effort**: diagnostic + light theory; MEDIUM (attention ≠ contribution); weeks.
- **prior_work**: 2609.03515 "ranking preservation" for decode-time KV eviction (one area only).
- **so_what**: turns the random-baseline crisis in two sub-areas into one measurable regime boundary. *Overlaps C7 in motivation (complementary: C7 = how much room, C8 = why).*

## C9. PTQ rounds away the cumulative RL delta
*S2.*
- **Method**: Parent→RL pairs (R1-Distill-1.5B → DeepScaleR-1.5B, ProRL v1/v2 checkpoints; R1-Distill-7B → Skywork-OR1-7B). Quantize both with GPTQ/AWQ/RTN at W8/W4/W3. Delta survival ratio ‖Q(W_rl) − Q(W_sft)‖ / ‖W_rl − W_sft‖; behaviour: accuracy, length, agreement with parent. "RL gain retained" vs bit-width and RL steps.
- **Hypothesis**: At W3/W4, RL gain retained ≪ base-capability retention (e.g. 40% vs 95%); quantized RL models drift toward parent behaviour; longer RL → larger deltas → more survives (opposite of pretraining over-training trend). Null (RL gains survive) also clean.
- **MVE**: R1-Distill-1.5B vs DeepScaleR-1.5B, RTN/GPTQ W4/W3, MATH-500 + AIME24 × 4. ~1.5 GPU-h.
- **Type / risk / effort**: empirical + diagnostic; MEDIUM (partly pre-empted: QuRL 2602.13953 per-step updates below quantization error; QuantLRM 2602.02581 uses fine-tuning delta to protect channels; 2511.15694 quantization × LRM RL); days–2 weeks.
- **so_what**: everyone ships quantized RL'd reasoners; if quantization silently reverts RL, deployment must protect the delta.

## C10. Drafter surprisal as a free KV-eviction signal for long reasoning
*S3.*
- **Method**: Run target with drafter (EAGLE-3 or small sibling); each decoded token gets a free accepted/rejected bit and drafter probability. Under fixed KV budget, protect prompt; evict most-predictable decoded tokens first (FlashAttention-compatible, no attention scores). Compare vs random, R-KV, H2O/SnapKV on AIME/MATH/GPQA.
- **Hypothesis**: Tokens a cheap model can predict carry little future-needed information → surprisal retention beats random at ≤10% budgets. If it ties random, strong principled support for Random Attention's regime claim.
- **MVE**: R1-Distill-7B + 1.5B drafter, offline teacher-forced surprisal, eviction at 2K/4K on 100 MATH + 30 AIME. ~2 GPU-h.
- **Type / risk / effort**: new method; HIGH (decode KV may be so redundant any rule ties random; predictable final numbers can still be critical); weeks.
- **prior_work**: LLMLingua / Selective-Context self-information (prompt side); no decode-KV use of SD signals found.
- **so_what**: zero-overhead composition of the two dominant decode-efficiency techniques.

## C11. Grounded-token agreement: a label-free metric for visual-token pruning
*S3.*
- **Method**: Full-token model generates long outputs (descriptions, visual CoT). Mark visually grounded tokens (log-prob drops > τ when image removed). Teacher-force through pruned variants (FastV, SparseVLM, VisionZip, DART, DivPrune, pooling, random, low-res) at 11–33%; top-1 agreement and expected accepted length on grounded tokens only. Check ranking vs hard-subset accuracy (VTC-Bench-hard, OCRBench, RefCOCO).
- **Hypothesis**: Whole-sequence agreement inflated by language prior; grounded-token agreement separates methods that benchmarks tie, exposes random's apparent strength as an artifact, predicts OCR/grounding collapse.
- **MVE**: Qwen2.5-VL-7B, 300 images, 5 pruners × 2 budgets, teacher forcing only. ~1.5 GPU-h.
- **Type / risk / effort**: diagnostic + benchmark; MEDIUM (grounded tokens sparse; may only reproduce hard-subset numbers); weeks.
- **prior_work**: flip/KL metrics 2407.09141 (text compression); SpecVLM 2509.11815 / HiViS 2509.23928 (pruned drafters, not evaluation).
- **so_what**: cheap dense evaluation for an overcrowded subfield.

## C12. Self-transcription: query-agnostic visual KV compression for multi-turn image reuse
*S3.*
- **Method**: Prefill image, teacher-force model's own exhaustive self-description (200–400 tokens, once per image); score each visual KV per head by max attention from transcription queries; keep top r%, drop transcription. Reuse compressed cache for 5–10 follow-up questions. Compare vs query-aware pruning fixed at Q1, coverage (DART/DivPrune), random, pooling; per-turn accuracy.
- **Hypothesis**: Query-aware visual pruning wins turn 1 and collapses on later turns (mirrors KVzip / query-visibility audit in text); self-transcription matches coverage on general and beats it on OCR/grounding.
- **MVE**: Qwen2.5-VL-7B, ~150 images × ≥5 questions, 10%/25%. ~2 GPU-h.
- **Type / risk / effort**: new method + empirical; MEDIUM (transcription attention may inherit position bias; untranscribed small text gets evicted); weeks.
- **prior_work**: KVzip 2505.23416 (text), RestoreKV 2608.01247, query-visibility audit 2607.11942; no visual version found.
- **so_what**: multi-turn chat over one image is the dominant MLLM usage; current pruning evaluation is single-turn.

## C13. Length-mediated compounding of quantization × KV eviction in reasoning models
*S3.*
- **Method**: 3×3 grid on R1-Distill-7B/1.5B: weights {BF16, AWQ-4, GPTQ-3} × KV {full, R-KV fixed budget, random decode eviction}; MATH-500/AIME24 × 4. Fit additive failure log-odds model, estimate interaction; mediation by trace-length bins; intervention with the overthinking penalty from 2606.00206; cost per solved problem.
- **Hypothesis**: Super-additive interaction, mostly mediated by trace length (quantized models think longer → more of trace past the eviction budget); length penalty restores composability.
- **MVE**: 1.5B 3×3 grid, 100 MATH-500, KV budget 2K. ~2 GPU-h.
- **Type / risk / effort**: empirical + simple fix; MEDIUM (may only appear at 3-bit); weeks.
- **prior_work**: 2606.00206 (quantized reasoners think longer), QAPruner 2604.02816 (token pruning amplifies W4A4 error); no quant × eviction interaction study found.
- **so_what**: production stacks combine both; "orthogonal" claims are untested on reasoning.
